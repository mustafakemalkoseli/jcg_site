Delete My Data

Lucky & Lucy · Just Creative Games

Lucky & Lucy has no user accounts. The only data we hold about a player is a leaderboard entry: a nickname, a random player ID and a best score. Everything else stays on your device.

Delete your leaderboard entry

  1. Send an email to info@justcreativegames.com with the subject “Delete my data – Lucky & Lucy”.
  2. Include your leaderboard nickname and your best score (so we can find the right entry).
  3. We delete the entry and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted: your nickname, player ID, best score and its timestamp. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Delete data on your device

Uninstall the Game. This removes your progress, settings, items and balances from the device. On Android you can also use Settings > Apps > Lucky & Lucy > Storage > Clear data.

Purchase records

Purchase history is held by Apple or Google, not by us. Manage it in your Apple or Google account.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
