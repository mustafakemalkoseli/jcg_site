Privacy Policy

Lucky & Lucy · Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: Lucky & Lucy has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you join the leaderboard, we store the nickname you choose, a random player ID and your best score. That is all we collect.

1. Who we are

Lucky & Lucy (the “Game”) is published by Just Creative Games (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact: info@justcreativegames.com.

2. What we collect

Your nickname and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as a nickname.

3. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising identifiers; the Game shows no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No payment card details.

4. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The Game receives only a confirmation of the purchase and a transaction ID, which stays on your device so that the same purchase is not delivered twice.

5. Service providers

The leaderboard database is hosted by Supabase, Inc., a cloud database provider, which processes the leaderboard data on our behalf. As with any internet service, the provider’s servers technically process your IP address when the Game connects; we do not store it or use it to identify you. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

6. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) applies, we process leaderboard data to provide the feature you asked for (performance of a contract) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a fair leaderboard.

7. How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is in operation, or until you ask us to delete yours. Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your leaderboard entry at any time, and you may object to or restrict its processing. See Delete My Data for the steps, or write to info@justcreativegames.com. We answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not ask for personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname, contact us and we will delete the entry.

10. International transfers

The leaderboard database may be located outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by our provider.

11. Security

Connections to the leaderboard are encrypted (HTTPS), and the database can only be read or written through limited functions. No system is perfectly secure, but the data we hold is minimal by design.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the Game or on this website.
