Support

Lucky & Lucy

Need help? Write to info@justcreativegames.com. Tell us your device model, the version of the Game and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the dog?

Open Settings and choose Controls: Swipe (default), Tap or Joystick. You can change it during a game from the pause menu.

I bought Golden Treats but did not receive them.

Close the Game completely and open it again with an internet connection; unfinished purchases are delivered at start-up. If they still do not arrive, email us with the date of purchase and the order number from your Apple or Google receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or from your order history in Google Play.

I reinstalled the Game and my items are gone.

Progress, items and balances are saved on your device only, so uninstalling removes them. We cannot restore them.

How do I change my leaderboard name or remove my score?

You can change the name in the Game. To remove your entry, see Delete My Data.

Where do I enter a gift code?

Tap the green + button at the top right of the main menu and type the code.

Does the Game have ads?

No.
